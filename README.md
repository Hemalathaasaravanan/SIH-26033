## Problem Title
SIH 26033: Multiple intermediaries reduce farmers earnings and increase consumer prices.

## Problem Creater's Organization
Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food & Public Distribution

## Theme
Agriculture, FoodTech & Rural Development

## Problem Description
Expected Solution: Create a digital marketplace that:

• Connects farmers/FPOs directly with consumers and bulk buyers.
• Provides logistics support.
• Uses AI for demand forecasting and route optimization.

Benefits:

• Better prices for farmers.
• Lower prices for consumers.
• Reduced supply chain inefficiencies.

## Proposed Solution
We propose creating a digital platform that connects farmers directly with consumers and buyers.

1. Farmers can upload their products and prices.
2. Buyers can directly purchase products from farmers.
3. AI predicts product demand and suggests prices.
4. Provides transportation and delivery support.
5. Tracks orders and online payments.
6. 
Innovation: AI-based demand prediction and smart delivery routes help reduce middlemen, increase farmers' income and provide affordable prices to consumers.

## Technical Approach
A. Frontend Development
*React.js – User-friendly website interface.
*HTML, CSS and JavaScript – Responsive design.
*Separate dashboards for farmers, buyers and administrators.

B. Backend Development
*Python with FastAPI or Django.
*MySQL/PostgreSQL – Store farmer, product, order and customer details.
*REST APIs – Connect frontend and backend.

C. AI and Machine Learning
*Demand Forecasting: Predict future demand using historical sales, seasonal trends and market data.
*Price Prediction: Suggest suitable selling prices based on market trends.
*Route Optimization: Find efficient delivery routes using location and distance data.
*Recommendation System: Recommend suitable buyers and products.

D. Logistics Management
*Connect farmers with transport providers.
*Track orders and delivery status.
*Group nearby orders to reduce transportation costs.

E. Payment and Security
*Secure online payment integration.
*User authentication and role-based access.
*Digital invoices and order history.
